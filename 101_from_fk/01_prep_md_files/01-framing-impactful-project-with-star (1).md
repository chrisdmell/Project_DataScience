**Claude:** Great project — lots of meat here. Let me frame it in STAR:

---

**Situation**
Flipkart spends significantly on Meta performance marketing, but ROI measurement relied on last-touch attribution — which inflates channel credit and gives a distorted picture of true incrementality. Leadership needed a reliable basis for budget decisions.

**Task**
Design and own a rigorous incrementality measurement framework to surface the true ROI of Meta spends — granular enough to be actionable at BU and category level.

**Action**
- Designed a holdout-based A/B experiment — sized the holdout at 8% by calculating the minimum detectable effect needed for statistical significance on customer conversion, evaluated across 2-week and 3-week windows given Flipkart's scale
- Published weekly results as they came in; achieved stat-sig on incremental customers in ~2 weeks, incremental GMV in ~3 weeks
- Broke results down by BU (since each BU owns its own spend), computing incremental customers, incremental GMV, and ROI per BU
- Identified underperforming BUs and drilled further into super-category level to pinpoint which SCs were dragging performance — enabling targeted corrective action rather than broad cuts
- Packaged findings and recommendations for the Business Performance team who controls campaign setup and targeting

**Result**
- Established ground truth on Meta ROI — moved the org away from inflated last-touch numbers
- Found the channel was healthy overall → justified scaling up Meta budgets
- Identified specific SC targeting corrections for underperforming BUs
- Directly influenced campaign strategy and budget allocation decisions

---

**A few things to sharpen in your delivery:**

1. **Lead with stakes** — open with the dollar magnitude if you can ("Flipkart spends X Cr/year on Meta") to anchor why this mattered
2. **Own the design decision** — the holdout sizing methodology (power analysis across windows) is sophisticated; don't undersell it, it shows rigor
3. **Quantify the result** — if you can say "budgets were scaled by X%" or "savings/reallocation of Y Cr in underperforming BUs," that's your punchline
4. **Avoid jargon without definition** — "stat-sig," "BU," "SC" are fine internally but in an interview, briefly define them once
